# Clinic dashboard proposal

This app is for clinic owners and practice managers who need a single place to track patients, appointments, and follow-up care without bouncing between spreadsheets and separate tools.

The two key questions behind the dashboard are: "Is the practice growing?" and "What share of appointments are no-shows?" The revenue and appointment-vitals views make those questions visible in one glance.

The design prioritizes fast triage and trust: patient records stay grouped by owner, the dashboard surface is simple, and the data model is built so a second user cannot see another user's records.
